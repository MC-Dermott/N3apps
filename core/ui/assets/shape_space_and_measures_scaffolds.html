Elapsed time number line

Count on (or back) along a number line, one jump at a time, to find the elapsed time.

Count on to the next whole hour first, then add whole hours, then the rest of the minutes.

Timetable highlighter

Step through a timetable cell by cell to find a journey time, or check each service against a deadline.

Find the row for your stop and the column for your service — the cell where they meet is the time.

Cuboid layer builder

Find the base area first, then build the volume up layer by layer.

Volume = length × width × height — find the base area, then multiply by the height.

Triangle area stepper

Multiply base by height, then halve it, to find the area of a triangle.

Area = ½ × base × height — the height is always measured at right angles to the base.

Rectangle area grid

See the rectangle as an array of unit squares, then multiply width by height to find the area.

Area = length × width — every little square in the grid is one square unit.

L-shape area splitter

Split the L-shape into two rectangles, find each area, then add them together.

Split into two rectangles, find each area, then add the two areas together.

Street walker

Walk down Main Street one junction at a time, counting the turnings as you go.

Count turnings on the side you were asked about only, in the order you reach them.
